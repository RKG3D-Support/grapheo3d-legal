# Privacy Policy — Grapheo 3D

**Last updated:** May 2026

## 1. Overview

Grapheo 3D ("the Application") is a desktop application for Windows 
developed by RKG3D.

This policy explains how the Application handles data. 
**The short version: Grapheo 3D does not collect, store, or transmit 
any personal data.**

---

## 2. Data Collection

Grapheo 3D does **not** collect any data of any kind, including but 
not limited to:

- Personal information (name, email address, location, etc.)
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- File contents processed by the application
- Device identifiers

---

## 3. Crash Reports

In the event of an unexpected application shutdown, Grapheo 3D may 
offer to help you send a crash report.

- **No data is transmitted automatically.** 
  The Application prepares a pre-filled email template containing 
  only technical diagnostic information (error logs, application 
  version, and operating system version).
- **You remain in full control.** The email is opened in your default 
  mail client and must be explicitly sent by you. 
  You can review and edit its content before sending.
- **No personal data is included** in crash reports unless you 
  voluntarily add it.
- Crash reports are sent directly to the developer's support address 
  and are used solely for debugging purposes.

---

## 4. Third-Party Components

Grapheo 3D uses the following third-party software components, 
which operate entirely locally on your device and do not transmit 
any data:

- **FFmpeg / FFprobe** — media processing (LGPL v2.1+)
- **MiDaS** — depth estimation models (MIT License, Intel ISL)
- **WPF UI** — user interface library (MIT License)

---

## 5. Microsoft Store

When you download Grapheo 3D through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft 
may collect diagnostic and purchase data in accordance with their own 
privacy policy. This is outside the scope of the Application itself.

Microsoft Privacy Statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Grapheo 3D does not knowingly collect any information from anyone 
under the age of 13.
